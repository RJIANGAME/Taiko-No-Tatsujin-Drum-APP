Android drum controller for Windows

Turn your phone into a taiko drum.

Tap blue rims and red drum zones on your phone. The PC receiver sends physical-key scan codes for D, F, J, and K.

Connection

Wi-Fi or USB debugging mode

The receiver shows your PC IP address. Type that IP into the Android app, keep port 27183, then tap Wi-Fi. For USB, run the ADB reverse helper and tap USB in the app.

  1. 1

    Double-click Taiko-Drum-Menu.bat.

  2. 2

    Choose Wi-Fi receiver, or admin receiver if the game needs it.

  3. 3

    Enter the shown PC IP in the phone app and play.
